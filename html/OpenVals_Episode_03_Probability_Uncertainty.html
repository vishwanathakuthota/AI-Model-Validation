A probability is not a promise. A confidence score is not automatically evidence of correctness.

7. Experiment — Base Rates Matter

Suppose prevalence is 1%, sensitivity is 95%, and specificity is 95%.

prevalence=.01; sensitivity=.95; specificity=.95
fpr=1-specificity
posterior=(sensitivity*prevalence)/(sensitivity*prevalence+fpr*(1-prevalence))
print(f"P(condition | positive) = {posterior:.2%}")

The base rate changes the posterior probability. Sensitivity alone does not determine it.

9. Threshold Trade-offs

rng=np.random.default_rng(7); n=2000
y=rng.binomial(1,.25,n)
s=np.where(y==1,rng.beta(5,2,n),rng.beta(2,5,n))
def rates(t):
    pred=(s>=t).astype(int)
    tp=((y==1)&(pred==1)).sum(); fn=((y==1)&(pred==0)).sum()
    fp=((y==0)&(pred==1)).sum(); tn=((y==0)&(pred==0)).sum()
    return tp/(tp+fn),fp/(fp+tn)
ts=np.linspace(0,1,101); rr=np.array([rates(t) for t in ts])
plt.figure(figsize=(8,5))
plt.plot(ts,rr[:,0],label="True Positive Rate")
plt.plot(ts,rr[:,1],label="False Positive Rate")
plt.xlabel("Decision threshold"); plt.ylabel("Rate")
plt.title("Threshold Changes Decision Behaviour")
plt.legend(); plt.grid(alpha=.25); plt.show()

11. Experiment — Calibration

rng=np.random.default_rng(123); n=10000
phat=rng.uniform(.02,.98,n); outcome=rng.binomial(1,phat)
bins=np.linspace(0,1,11); idx=np.digitize(phat,bins)-1
mp=[]; obs=[]
for i in range(10):
    mask=idx==i
    if mask.sum():
        mp.append(phat[mask].mean()); obs.append(outcome[mask].mean())
plt.figure(figsize=(7,6))
plt.plot([0,1],[0,1],linestyle="--",label="Perfect calibration")
plt.plot(mp,obs,marker="o",label="Synthetic model")
plt.xlabel("Mean predicted probability"); plt.ylabel("Observed positive frequency")
plt.title("Calibration Curve"); plt.legend(); plt.grid(alpha=.25); plt.show()

13. Sources of Uncertainty

Aleatoric uncertainty concerns variability inherent in the data-generating process.

Epistemic uncertainty concerns limited knowledge, finite data, or uncertainty about model representation.

This is a conceptual summary rather than a universal identity.
